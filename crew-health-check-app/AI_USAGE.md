## AI inside the product

Each model gets a line here and a model card when it is built (ARCHITECTURE.md section 26):

- `sep-risk`: **trained in A1 and running in the app since A2 (15 November 2026).** A logistic regression on eight features from NASA DONKI flare and CME records. In the browser it scores a flare and can raise one watch-level alert, labeled "Model estimate" and shown as an "Experimental model score" out of 100. It never raises an act-level alert or changes who leads. Its results, limits and label policy are in the model card, [ml/reports/sep-risk-v1.md](ml/reports/sep-risk-v1.md), and on the app's model card screen. It is a machine-learning model inside the product, built by code that Claude Code wrote.
- `personal-pattern`: **built in A3 (15 November 2026).** A personal pattern check on each crew member's own daily readings: it learns on board, from that person's baseline days, how their readings usually move together (Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage, squared Mahalanobis distance) and shows an insight when today's combination is more unusual than every baseline day. It only shows insights, tagged "Model estimate": it never sets a status, never creates or hides an alert, never changes a route and never sends a message by itself. **It was evaluated only on simulated data** from this project's simulator, against the rules alone and scikit-learn's IsolationForest; the results and limits are in [ml/reports/personal-pattern-v1.md](ml/reports/personal-pattern-v1.md) and on the app's model card screen. They show that the method works as designed on the simulator, not that it notices real illness.
- `earth-hospital`: **built in A4 (15 November 2026), after Divyanshu brought it back into the submission.** A logistic regression on five changes from a person's own baseline (heart rate, temperature, blood pressure upper and lower, glucose), trained on Earth ICU patients of one hospital system from the PhysioNet 2019 challenge and tested once on a second. It only shows insights, tagged "Model estimate" and "Earth ICU data", with the caveat "Learned from Earth hospital patients. Never tested on astronauts. A second opinion only."; the crew card shows no number and names no disease. Its results, including that it does not beat temperature change alone, are in [ml/reports/earth-hospital-v1.md](ml/reports/earth-hospital-v1.md).
